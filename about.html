About

I'm Abdul (Abdulrasheed Ibrahim), a software engineer with 10+ years of experience building systems in Python, Rust, and TypeScript. I'm currently Head of Engineering at Convexity, where I work on cNGN, a compliant Naira stablecoin, and the infrastructure around it.

I contribute to CPython and write about the internals I learn along the way. Earlier this year my talk From Zero to CPython: Contributing to the Core from Where No Community Exists was accepted at the PyCon US 2026 Maintainers Summit.

Previously: FoundryAI (San Francisco), CTO at MyHives (The Hague), Isofy (US), and Head of Engineering at Zarttech. I'm also finishing an MSc in AI & Robotics at the University of Hertfordshire. I live in the UK.
